Delete your Bateekh Man account

Bateekh Man by YH Games for Fun · Last updated: 4 November 2026

You can delete your Bateekh Man account and its data at any time, in the game or by email.

In the game (quickest)

  1. Open Bateekh Man and go to SETTINGS.
  2. Press DELETE (the red button at the bottom).
  3. Type YES in the box, then press DELETE.

You need an internet connection. Your account and everything of yours on our server are deleted at once, and the game starts again with a new, empty account.

Without the game

Email yhgamesforfun@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Bateekh Man account" and your PLAYER ID (at the bottom of the SETTINGS page). We reply to confirm, and delete the account within 30 days.

If you no longer have the game but bought something in it, send us the order number from the store's receipt instead (on Google Play it starts with GPA.; on the App Store it is in your Apple receipt), and we will find your account that way. If you never bought anything, the account holds no name, email address or phone number, so we cannot tell which account was yours.

What is deleted

  • your player ID and your sign-in session;
  • your progress: levels won and records, melons, lives, power-ups, outfits, VIP, the 7-day gift, achievements, golden melons, daily challenge results and endless best;
  • your runs and the history of your melons;
  • the link between you and your purchases.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Store order records: the store, order number, product, and the purchase and refund dates, kept without any link to you or your account. We keep them so a purchase is never delivered twice, so you can still restore VIP or an outfit from the same Google or Apple account, to deal with refunds and fraud, and for tax and accounting.
  • Our server provider's logs (IP address, user agent, approximate location): deleted automatically within 7 days.
  • Database backups: replaced within 7 days.
  • Google and Apple keep their own records of your purchases, under their own privacy policies.

Deleting cannot be undone, and it does not refund purchases (see the Refund Policy). More about your data: Privacy Policy.
