Bateekh Man: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Bateekh Man is a game by YH Games for Fun, an individual developer in Egypt ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game keeps about you, why, and for how long. Questions: yhgamesforfun@gmail.com.

In short

  • The game makes an anonymous account for you. It never asks for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Our server keeps your progress and your purchases, so the game works and nobody can cheat them.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. We never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account in the game at any time: SETTINGS → DELETE.

1. What we keep, and why

WhatWhy
Your player ID: a random code made the first time the game connects to our server. It is your anonymous account.To know which progress and purchases are yours.
Your progress: the levels you have won, your best melons and times for each difficulty, your melons (the game's currency) and a history of how you got and spent them, your lives, power-ups, outfits and VIP, your step in the 7-day gift, the achievements and golden melons you have collected, your daily challenge results and your best endless distance.To run the game and keep your progress safe. Our server works these out itself, so they cannot be faked.
Your runs: for each game you start, the mode, level and difficulty, when it started and ended, and the result (melons, win or loss, hits, distance, and whether our checks flagged it).To pay out melons fairly and to stop cheating.
What happens in a level: where on the level you were hit and by what, where you fell and why, when you continued or went back to a checkpoint, the checkpoints you reached, when you left the level, and your win; each with the level's clock.To find the parts of the levels that are too hard or unclear, and make them better.
Your purchases: the store (Google Play or the App Store), the order number, the product, the date, and the refund date if there is one.To give you what you paid for, to deliver each purchase only once, to restore VIP and outfits on a new device, and to take a purchase back if the store refunds it.
Technical data: your IP address and the short "user agent" text your device sends with every request. Our server provider's request logs also record the approximate location (such as country and city) that its network works out from the IP address.Every online service receives these. They are kept for security and to fix problems. We do not use them to find out where you are.

We get the details of a purchase from Google or Apple when they confirm it. We never see your card or bank details: Google and Apple handle all payments.

Kept only on your device: your settings (language, difficulty, picture quality), the keys that keep you signed in to your anonymous account, a copy of your wallet so the menu opens quickly, and results waiting to be sent while you are offline.

The share card: when you press SHARE after a win, the game makes a picture of your result on your device and opens your phone's share menu. On Android the picture is also saved in your gallery; on a computer it is saved in your Pictures folder. We never receive it.

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to help you, and keep it only as long as we need it for that.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not show ads, and we do not use your device's advertising ID.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and we do not follow you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • The game does not read your contacts, photos or location, and does not use your camera or microphone.

3. Who handles the data for us

  • Our cloud server provider runs our server for us: the anonymous accounts, the database and its logs. Our database is stored in Europe. The provider works only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement. Like most cloud providers, it and the companies it works with may reach the data from other countries, such as the United States, for support and operations, under the European Union's Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store sell the game's items and take the payments, under their own terms and privacy policies. Our server asks them to confirm each purchase.

We do not share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

5. Deleting your account

  • In the game: SETTINGS → DELETE, type YES, then press DELETE. You need an internet connection. Your account and everything of yours on our server are deleted at once.
  • Without the game: email yhgamesforfun@gmail.com with your PLAYER ID (at the bottom of the SETTINGS page). We delete the account within 30 days.
  • All the details: https://youssef-haitham.github.io/bateekhman/delete-account.html

Deleting cannot be undone, and it does not refund purchases.

6. Children

Bateekh Man is for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly keep data about children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, email us with the PLAYER ID and we will delete the account.

7. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of the data we keep about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Where your law gives you more rights (for example the GDPR in the European Union and the UK: to object, to restrict how we use your data, and to take it elsewhere), you have them too. Email us with your PLAYER ID, because it is the only way to find an anonymous account; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

For players in the European Union and the UK, our legal reasons are: providing the game you asked for (your account, progress and purchases); our legitimate interest in keeping the game secure and fair (runs and logs) and in making the levels better (what happens in a level); and the law (order records for tax and accounting).

8. Security

The game talks to our server only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Nobody can read or change the database directly: the game can only make a small set of requests that the server checks, and each one reaches only your own account. Every purchase is checked with Google or Apple before anything is given.

9. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we post the new version here with a new date. If a change is important, we will also tell you in the game or on its store page.
