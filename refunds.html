Bateekh Man: Refund Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Everything you buy in Bateekh Man is bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store. They take the payment, so refunds follow their rules. Nothing here limits your rights under the consumer law of your country.

How to ask for a refund

  • iPhone and iPad (App Store): Apple decides every refund, and we cannot refund App Store purchases ourselves. Ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Android (Google Play): in the first 48 hours after buying, you can usually ask Google Play directly: see Google Play's refund help. After that, email us with the order number, which starts with GPA. and is in Google's receipt email. We look at every request, and we refund through Google Play when a purchase did not arrive or something went wrong on our side.

What happens when a purchase is refunded

When Google or Apple tells our server that a purchase was refunded or cancelled, the game takes back what that purchase gave:

  • a melon pack: the same number of melons. If you have already spent some of them, the missing melons become a debt: your new melons pay it first, and power-ups cannot be used until it is paid;
  • lives, and the unlimited-lives time the purchase added;
  • VIP, with the melons and lives it gave, or the outfit the purchase gave.

If something did not arrive

A purchase is delivered as soon as the store confirms it to our server. If you paid and did not get it, open the game with the internet on and wait a moment. On a phone, you can also press RESTORE in SETTINGS to get back VIP and outfits. If it still has not arrived, email yhgamesforfun@gmail.com with the store's order number and your PLAYER ID (at the bottom of the SETTINGS page), and we will put it right.

Deleting your account

Deleting your account does not refund anything.
